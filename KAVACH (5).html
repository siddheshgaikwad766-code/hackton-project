LEARN


What is PII?
Personally identifiable information is anything that identifies you alone or combined with other data: ID numbers, phone, email, address, bank details, date of birth.


How to protect your Aadhaar
Prefer Masked Aadhaar or a Virtual ID when a full number is not legally required, lock biometrics when unused, and never share an OTP.


How to spot a scam
Urgency, threats, prizes that need fees, requests for OTP/PIN/CVV, lookalike links, and remote-access apps are the usual red flags. Stop, do not click, verify via an official channel.


What should you redact?
ID numbers, account and card numbers, phone, email, address and signatures, unless the recipient has a verified, specific need.


Sharing documents safely
Write the purpose on the copy (e.g. "for X only, date"), send over a trusted channel, and share the protected copy rather than the original.
